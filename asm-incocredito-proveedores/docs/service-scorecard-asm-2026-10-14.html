Methodology

Each facet is graded A–F against a fixed rubric and converted to points (A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1, F=0). The overall grade is the weighted mean of those points, mapped back to a letter at ≥4.5 A, ≥3.5 B, ≥2.5 C, ≥1.5 D, ≥0.5 E, else F. Weights favour the facets that drive maintenance cost and discoverability. Facets marked N/A are excluded from both sides of the average.
